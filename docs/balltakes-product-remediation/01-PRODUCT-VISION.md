# Product Vision

## Positioning
Ball Takes should evolve into a football-content intelligence platform built around predictions, pundit comparison, receipts and AI-assisted content creation.

## Core User Journey

### Discover
The homepage hooks users with an active rolling feed of:
- memes;
- GIFs;
- trending football banter;
- pundit receipts;
- community prediction receipts;
- hot takes;
- match events;
- exact-score wins;
- funny misses.

### Follow
Users choose:
- teams;
- competitions;
- pundits;
- creators or analysts they want to compare themselves against.

### Predict
Users create:
- match winner predictions;
- score predictions;
- season predictions;
- player award predictions;
- selected football takes.

### Compare
Ball Takes compares:
- user prediction;
- selected pundit predictions or quotes;
- final result;
- relevant match/player/team data.

### Receipt
Every meaningful prediction/take becomes a persistent record.

A receipt should contain enough structured context to be reused later by Studio.

### Banter
The system identifies funny, dramatic or interesting story angles from the receipt.

### Studio
Studio converts structured football context into:
- short video scripts;
- podcast segments;
- meme concepts;
- captions;
- Instagram carousel text;
- X threads;
- commentary scripts;
- AI video prompts;
- AI image prompts;
- voice-over prompts;
- complete content packs.

### Export
Users copy/export the generated pack to external AI/media platforms such as:
- ChatGPT;
- Sora;
- Canva;
- CapCut;
- Runway;
- ElevenLabs;
- other current or future tools.

## Primary Product Pillars
1. Predict
2. Pundits
3. Receipts
4. Banter
5. Studio
6. Leagues/Aura

## Homepage Principle
The homepage should demonstrate the product rather than explain it at length.

A visitor should quickly see:
- football content;
- funny memes/GIFs;
- pundit/user comparisons;
- current fixtures;
- a clear Studio pathway.
