# Aura, Leagues & Retention

## Aura
Use Aura as the primary user-facing reputation/scoring concept where possible.
Avoid competing user-facing point currencies unless there is a strong business reason.

Audit existing scoring before renaming or changing rules.

## Aura UX
Show:
- total Aura;
- weekly change;
- streak;
- league rank;
- percentile where data supports it;
- achievements/tier if implemented.

## Leagues
Keep and strengthen.

Core league flows:
- create;
- invite;
- join;
- leaderboard;
- weekly movement;
- friend comparison;
- league-specific receipts;
- Studio stories generated from league rivalries.

## Retention Hooks
- unfinished picks;
- result receipts;
- pundit comparison results;
- rank changes;
- weekly recap;
- season recap;
- Studio content suggestions.
